The licence terms themselves are NOT recorded: if they require an author credit (e.g. CC BY), add it here. Converted for the game with three.js r128 (FBXLoader + GLTFExporter): units/axes fixed, duplicate glass shells removed, vertices welded, parts split with their own pivots (propellers, wheels, ailerons, elevator), textures reduced to 1024 px (exterior) and 512 px (interior) JPEG, scaled 1.5x. No other change to the design.
- `assets/models/vehicles/ducati.glb` — **Ducati Lenovo Team - MotoGP26** by **Kenny3D89** ([Sketchfab source](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ducati-lenovo-team-motogp26-497ac78767344110a0046a3ab4a5d058)), licensed **CC BY 4.0** ([license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)). Used by `vehicles.js` as the drivable motorbike. Attribution: "Ducati Lenovo Team - MotoGP26" by Kenny3D89, via Sketchfab, CC BY 4.0. Changes made for the game: the paddock stand under the tail was removed, node transforms baked into the vertices, the two wheels split out as pivot nodes, polygons reduced (87k -> 33k triangles), textures resized to <= 768 px JPEG (PNG where alpha is needed), glass made alpha-blended, unsupported glTF extensions dropped. The livery shows real sponsor marks (Ducati, Lenovo) from the original model.

- `assets/textures/tee_capybara_front.jpg`, `assets/textures/tee_capybara_splash.jpg` — the "Capybara tee" print (`look.js`). Cut and perspective-corrected from two photos of the project owner's own T-shirt supplied by the owner on 2026-11-11; the shirt's graphic (a capybara in sunglasses, "CAPIBARA") is a commercial garment design whose author and licence are NOT known, so no licence is recorded: replace it if that matters. No face is in these textures.
- Face photo (`look.js`): the player's own photo is processed on their device and kept in the browser's localStorage only; nothing is uploaded and no personal photo is stored in this repository.

- `sfx.js` — three interface sounds (a whoosh, a "tching" ping, a tick) cut from a recording supplied by the project owner (`videoplayback.m4b`), re-encoded to 22 kHz mono WAV and embedded as base64; no separate audio files.
- `assets/audio/manifest.json` — replacement slots only. No recorded engine, propeller, turbine, rotor, reactor, or sonar assets are shipped or loaded yet; the current vehicle loops are generated with the Web Audio API. Add a source and verified CC0/public-domain attribution here before adding any recording.

Everything else in the game (buildings, cars, characters, interiors) is procedural geometry generated in code, with no model files.

- `textures/bark_platanus.jpg` — "Bark Platanus", Poly Haven (polyhaven.com/a/bark_platanus), CC0 public domain; tree bark.

- The yacht, Jungle Island (terrain, vegetation, animals), train interiors and timetable boards, aircraft (airliner, fighter), Odesa Airport and the companion character are procedural geometry written for this game; no third-party model files.
