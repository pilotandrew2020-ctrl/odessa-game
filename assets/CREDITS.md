## 3D models
- `assets/models/props/ArmChair_01.glb`, `Television_01.glb`, `russian_food_cans_01.glb`, `hanging_picture_frame_02.glb`, `ClassicNightstand_01.glb`, `wooden_display_shelves_01.glb`, `cassette_player.glb`, `brass_vase_03.glb` — from **Poly Haven** (https://polyhaven.com/models), licensed **CC0** (public domain).
  Used for the armchairs in the flats, the 80s CRT television in the Soviet flat, the Soviet tins in its kitchen the framed painting on the lounge wall the bedside table by the sofa-bed the cube shelf in the modern flat the cassette recorder by the Soviet TV and the brass vase on the dinner table.
- `assets/models/apache.glb` — helicopter model that was already in the project when the asset programme started; source and licence not recorded.

- `sfx.js` — three interface sounds (a whoosh, a "tching" ping, a tick) cut from a recording supplied by the project owner (`videoplayback.m4b`), re-encoded to 22 kHz mono WAV and embedded as base64; no separate audio files.

Everything else in the game (buildings, cars, characters, interiors) is procedural geometry generated in code, with no model files.
