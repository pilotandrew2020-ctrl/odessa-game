My Odesa

Every street and building of Odesa from the real map, with real terrain. You start in Andrii's yard on Chykalenka St (formerly Williams), 59k. Go into the stairwell and the flat, the supermarket, the pharmacy, the Opera House and the railway station, ride the tram and walk around with a speaker. Play together with friends in co-op.

No account needed: one of you presses Create room, the other types the code and presses Join.
