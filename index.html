Welcome to Odesa

Every street and building of Odesa from the real map, with real terrain. You start in Andrii's yard on Chykalenka St (formerly Williams), 59k. Go into the stairwell and the flat, the supermarket, the pharmacy, the Opera House and the railway station, ride the tram and walk around with a speaker. Play together with friends in co-op.

Press Play to design your character, then step outside.

Controls

WASD walk · Shift run · Space jump · E action (enter, sit, tram) · F scooter · C call / leave your car (H — horn) · U or the Garage button: a turntable with every car, the Lamborghini, G-Wagon, bicycle and submarine — pick one and it is brought to you (a friend can ride along — press E / F beside the car) · O or the View button: camera view (third person · first person · over the shoulder · far · top-down; in a car: chase · cockpit · bonnet) · V helicopter (nose follows the camera · W/S fly · A/D strafe · Space/Shift up·down · Q/E turn) · B boat / J jet ski at the shore · Q speaker · M map · R rain on/off · T chat · G go to friend · mouse turns the camera
Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Terrain: SRTM (NASA). The yard, station, opera, sea terminal, stairs and parks were hand-built from photos.
